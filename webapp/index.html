About OpenTransition

Version: 1.0.0 (Web)

OpenTransition is a transition tracking application made specifically for transgender people, focusing on photo tracking and milestone management.

This web version provides the same core features as the Android app, with all data stored locally on your device for maximum privacy.

Features

  • 📸 Photo tracking (face, body, custom areas)
  • 🎯 Milestone management
  • 🖼️ Gallery view with filtering
  • 🔒 Privacy-focused with app lock
  • 💾 Export and import your data
  • 📱 Works offline as a Progressive Web App
  • 🔄 Import backups from the Android app

Privacy

All your photos and data are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server unless you explicitly choose to export your data.
