OpenTransition is a transition tracking app made specifically for transgender people. It helps you privately document your journey through photo tracking, milestone recording, and voice sample logging — all stored locally on your device.

OpenTransition is the active successor to the original TransTracks app, which was retired from the Play Store in 2025. If you were a TransTracks user, OpenTransition can import your existing .ttbackup data files.

FEATURES

📸 Photo Tracking
Organize your progress photos by type (face, body, custom). Browse and compare them over time in a clear gallery view. The built-in camera uses ML Kit face detection to help frame face photos consistently.

🎯 Milestone Management
Record and celebrate important moments in your journey — medical appointments, personal achievements, and anything else that matters to you.

🎙️ Voice Recording
Log voice samples over time to track changes in your voice. Recordings are organized by date alongside your photos.

🔒 Privacy First
Everything stays on your device. App lock (PIN, pattern, or biometric) keeps your data private. The Quick Hide feature lets you instantly conceal the app when needed and prevents screenshots.

⌚ Wear OS Companion (Coming Soon)
An Android smartwatch companion app is included. Capability integration and milestone sync are in active development.

💾 Import from TransTracks
Fully compatible with .ttbackup backup files from the original TransTracks app. Import all your photos, milestones, and audio recordings with no data loss.

🎨 Customizable Themes
Multiple color themes including pink, blue, purple, and green, with Material You dynamic color support on Android 12+.

PRIVACY & DATA SAFETY
• All photos, milestones, and audio recordings are stored only on your device
• Optional Firebase account sync for settings only (not photos or milestones)
• No personal data is sold or shared with third parties
• See our Privacy Policy for full details

OpenTransition is free and open source (GPL v3). Contributions are welcome at github.com/shelbeely/OpenTransition.
