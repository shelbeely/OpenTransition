# VistaPrint Custom Flyer Printing Guide

## For VistaPrint Custom Size Flyers (5.5" × 8.5")

This guide explains how to print the OpenTransition Printable Journal using **VistaPrint's custom flyer printing service** on 5.5" × 8.5" half-letter size paper.

## 📐 Specifications

### VistaPrint Custom Flyer Format
- **Paper Size**: 5.5" × 8.5" (Half-Letter / Statement Size)
- **Page Size with Bleed**: 5.75" × 8.75" (includes 0.125" bleed)
- **Format**: Individual flyer pages (not a booklet)
- **Color**: Full color (CMYK)
- **Paper Options**: 
  - 80 lb gloss text
  - 100 lb gloss cover
  - 14 pt cardstock (premium)
- **Finishing**: Optional UV coating, matte, or uncoated

### Why Custom Flyers at 5.5" × 8.5"?
This size is perfect for the journal because:
- ✅ Compact and portable - fits in bags easily
- ✅ Half the size of standard paper - economical
- ✅ Individual pages - organize in binders or bind custom
- ✅ High quality VistaPrint printing with vivid colors
- ✅ 3-hole punch option available
- ✅ Perfect for journaling and tracking

## 🖨️ Step-by-Step: Order from VistaPrint

### Step 1: Generate Your PDF

1. Open `index.html` in **Google Chrome** (recommended)
2. Press `Ctrl+P` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+P` (Mac)
3. Configure settings:
   - **Destination**: Save as PDF
   - **Paper size**: Custom → **5.75 × 8.75 inches** (with bleed)
   - **Margins**: None
   - **Scale**: 100%
   - **Background graphics**: ✓ **ENABLED** (crucial!)
   - **Headers/footers**: Disabled
4. Save as `OpenTransition-Journal-VistaPrint.pdf`

### Step 2: Upload to VistaPrint

1. **Visit**: [vistaprint.com/marketing-materials/flyers](https://www.vistaprint.com/marketing-materials/flyers)
2. **Click**: "Upload your own design"
3. **Select size**: **Custom Size** → Enter 5.5" × 8.5"
4. **Upload**: Your PDF file (5.75" × 8.75" with bleed)
5. **Quantity**: Choose how many sets (1 set = all ~30 pages)

### Step 3: Configure Options

**Paper & Finish:**
- **Budget**: 80 lb gloss text (~$0.15-0.20/page)
- **Standard**: 100 lb gloss cover (~$0.20-0.25/page)
- **Premium**: 14 pt cardstock (~$0.30-0.40/page)

**Finishing Options:**
- **UV Coating**: Shiny, fingerprint resistant
- **Matte**: Easy to write on, no glare (recommended for journaling)
- **Uncoated**: Most natural, best for writing notes

**Additional Services:**
- **3-Hole Punch**: Perfect for 5.5" × 8.5" 3-ring binders ($1-2 extra)
- **Rounded Corners**: Optional aesthetic touch
- **Shrink Wrapping**: Keeps sets organized

### Step 4: Review & Order

1. **Preview**: Check all pages look correct
2. **Verify**: Bleed extends to edges, size is 5.5" × 8.5"
3. **Quantity**: Adjust number of copies
4. **Checkout**: Place your order

**Typical turnaround**: 3-5 business days + shipping

## 📋 Organizing Your Printed Pages

Since you're printing individual flyer pages (not a bound booklet), here's how to organize them:

### Option 1: 5.5" × 8.5" 3-Ring Binder (Recommended)
1. Order with **3-hole punch** option from VistaPrint
2. Purchase a **5.5" × 8.5" junior-size 3-ring binder** (Amazon, Staples)
3. Insert pages directly - no hole punching needed!
4. Add divider tabs for different sections

**Benefits:**
- Perfect fit for half-letter pages
- Can add/remove pages easily
- Can print more tracking pages as needed
- Professional appearance
- Portable and compact

### Option 2: DIY Binding
1. Order pages without holes
2. Bring to a local print shop for binding:
   - **Spiral binding**: ~$3-5
   - **Coil binding**: ~$3-5  
   - **Comb binding**: ~$2-4
   - **Saddle-stitch**: ~$2-3 (stapled booklet)

### Option 3: Folder or Portfolio
1. Keep loose pages in a 5.5" × 8.5" folder
2. Use paper clips or binder clips for sections
3. Most flexible option

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Your file doesn't match our size"
- VistaPrint expects 5.5" × 8.5" final size
- Our file is 5.75" × 8.75" (includes bleed)
- **Solution**: Select "I've included bleed" during upload, specify trim to 5.5" × 8.5"

### "This size costs more than standard"
- Custom sizes may have premium pricing
- Still economical at ~$6-10 per journal
- **Solution**: Wait for VistaPrint sales (40-50% off)

### "Colors look washed out in preview"
- VistaPrint preview compresses images
- Final print will be vivid
- **Solution**: Proceed with order, actual print quality is excellent

### "Text near edges will be cut off"
- Check that important content is 0.5" from trim edge
- The bleed area (0.125") will be trimmed
- **Solution**: Our CSS already handles safe margins

### "File upload failed"
- PDF may be too large
- **Solution**: 
  1. Use Chrome's "Save as PDF" (creates smaller files)
  2. Or compress PDF at smallpdf.com
  3. Maximum file size: 100 MB
