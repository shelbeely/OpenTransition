# VistaPrint DIY Flyer System

## Build Your Own Custom Journal from Individual Flyers

This guide explains how to create a **personalized OpenTransition Journal** using VistaPrint's flyer printing service. Since VistaPrint requires a minimum of 25 copies per page design, this is a **DIY system** where you select which page types you want, order them as individual flyers, and build your own custom journal.

## 📐 Specifications

### VistaPrint Custom Flyer Format
- **Paper Size**: 5.5" × 8.5" (Half-Letter / Statement Size)
- **Page Size with Bleed**: 5.75" × 8.75" (includes 0.125" bleed)
- **Format**: Individual flyer pages (not a booklet)
- **Color**: Full color (CMYK)
- **Paper Options**: 
  - 80 lb gloss text
  - 100 lb gloss cover
  - 14 pt cardstock (premium)
- **Finishing**: Optional UV coating, matte, or uncoated

### How the DIY System Works
**VistaPrint Requirement:** Minimum of 25 copies per page design

**Your Solution:**
1. **Choose** which page types you want (photo tracking, milestones, calendars, etc.)
2. **Order** 25+ copies of each page type as individual flyers
3. **Build** your own journal by selecting the pages you need from each batch
4. **Share or save** extra copies for future use or to give to friends

**Benefits:**
- ✅ **Personalized:** Only order the page types you'll actually use
- ✅ **Bulk savings:** 25+ flyers per page type = lower cost per page
- ✅ **Flexible:** Mix and match pages to create your perfect journal
- ✅ **Extras:** Keep spare pages for future tracking or share with community
- ✅ **Quality:** Professional VistaPrint printing with vivid colors
- ✅ **Size:** Compact 5.5" × 8.5" - portable and convenient

## 🖨️ Step-by-Step: Order from VistaPrint

### Step 1: Choose Your Page Types

Decide which page types you want to order. Common choices:
- **Photo Tracking Pages** (face, body, or custom areas) - most popular
- **Milestone Pages** (medical, legal, social)
- **Monthly Calendar Pages** - reusable template
- **Reflection Pages** - for journaling
- **Notes Pages** - blank for free-form writing

**Remember:** VistaPrint requires **minimum 25 copies** of each page design, so choose wisely!

### Step 2: Generate PDFs for Each Page Type

For each page type you selected:

1. Open `index.html` in **Google Chrome** (recommended)
2. Press `Ctrl+P` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+P` (Mac)
3. Configure settings:
   - **Destination**: Save as PDF
   - **Paper size**: Custom → **5.75 × 8.75 inches** (with bleed)
   - **Margins**: None
   - **Scale**: 100%
   - **Background graphics**: ✓ **ENABLED** (crucial!)
   - **Pages**: Select the specific page range (e.g., pages 5-7 for face photos)
4. Save as descriptive name (e.g., `photo-face-tracking.pdf`, `monthly-calendar.pdf`)

**Pro Tip:** Print just 1-2 page types initially to test the system before ordering all types.

### Step 3: Upload Each Page Type to VistaPrint

For each page design PDF:

1. **Visit**: [vistaprint.com/marketing-materials/flyers](https://www.vistaprint.com/marketing-materials/flyers)
2. **Click**: "Upload your own design"
3. **Select size**: **5.5" × 8.5"** (Standard half-letter)
4. **Upload**: Your PDF file for this page type
5. **Quantity**: Minimum **25 copies** (or more if desired)

### Step 4: Configure Options for Each Page Type

**Paper & Finish:**
- **Budget**: 80 lb gloss text (~$0.15-0.20/flyer)
- **Standard**: 100 lb gloss cover (~$0.20-0.25/flyer) - **RECOMMENDED**
- **Premium**: 14 pt cardstock (~$0.30-0.40/flyer)

**Finishing Options:**
- **Matte**: Easy to write on, no glare (**RECOMMENDED** for journaling)
- **UV Coating**: Shiny, fingerprint resistant
- **Uncoated**: Most natural, best for writing notes

**Additional Services:**
- **3-Hole Punch**: Perfect for 5.5" × 8.5" 3-ring binders ($1-2 extra per set)
- **Rounded Corners**: Optional aesthetic touch
- **Shrink Wrapping**: Keeps 25-copy sets organized

### Step 5: Review & Place Orders

1. **Preview**: Check each page design looks correct
2. **Verify**: Bleed extends to edges, size is 5.5" × 8.5"
3. **Quantity**: Confirm 25 copies minimum per page type
4. **Checkout**: Place separate orders for each page type (or add all to cart)

**Typical turnaround**: 3-5 business days + shipping

### Step 6: Assemble Your Custom Journal

When your flyers arrive:
1. **Unwrap** each page type set (25 copies per design)
2. **Select** how many of each page you want in your journal (e.g., 5 photo pages, 2 calendar pages)
3. **Organize** your selected pages in the order you prefer
4. **Bind** using 3-ring binder, spiral binding, or folder
5. **Store extras** for future use or share with friends

**You've just created a custom journal exactly how YOU want it!** 🎉

## 📋 Building Your DIY Journal

Since you're receiving 25+ copies of each page type, here's how to assemble your journals:

### Step 1: Select Your Pages

From each 25-copy set, choose how many you want in your journal:
- **Photo tracking**: 3-5 pages
- **Monthly calendar**: 1-2 pages  
- **Milestones**: 2-3 pages
- **Notes/Reflection**: 3-5 pages
- **Total**: ~15-25 pages per journal

### Step 2: Organize Pages

Arrange your selected pages in your preferred order:
1. Cover/intro (if created)
2. Photo tracking pages
3. Milestone pages
4. Calendar pages
5. Reflection/Notes pages

### Step 3: Choose Binding Method

#### Option 1: 5.5" × 8.5" 3-Ring Binder (Recommended)
1. If you ordered with **3-hole punch** - pages are ready!
2. Purchase a **5.5" × 8.5" junior-size 3-ring binder** (Amazon, Staples, ~$6-12)
3. Insert pages directly
4. Add divider tabs for different sections

**Benefits:**
- Perfect fit for half-letter pages
- Can swap pages easily
- Can add more pages later from your extras
- Professional appearance
- Most versatile option

#### Option 2: DIY Binding
1. If ordered without holes, bring to a local print shop:
   - **Spiral binding**: ~$3-5 per journal
   - **Coil binding**: ~$3-5 per journal
   - **Comb binding**: ~$2-4 per journal
   - **Saddle-stitch**: ~$2-3 per journal (stapled)

#### Option 3: Simple Folder or Clip
1. Keep pages in a 5.5" × 8.5" folder
2. Use binder clips or paper clips
3. Most affordable and flexible

### Step 4: Create Multiple Journals or Share Extras

**You have enough for multiple journals!**

With 25 copies of each page type:
- Make 3-5 journals for yourself (for different time periods)
- Gift pre-assembled journals to friends
- Share extra pages with local support groups
- Keep spares for future tracking needs
- **Consider selling premade journals** to support your community! 💝

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Your file doesn't match our size"
- VistaPrint expects 5.5" × 8.5" final size
- Our file is 5.75" × 8.75" (includes bleed)
- **Solution**: Select "I've included bleed" during upload, specify trim to 5.5" × 8.5"

### "This size costs more than standard"
- Custom sizes may have premium pricing
- Still economical at ~$6-10 per journal
- **Solution**: Wait for VistaPrint sales (40-50% off)

### "Colors look washed out in preview"
- VistaPrint preview compresses images
- Final print will be vivid
- **Solution**: Proceed with order, actual print quality is excellent

### "Text near edges will be cut off"
- Check that important content is 0.5" from trim edge
- The bleed area (0.125") will be trimmed
- **Solution**: Our CSS already handles safe margins

### "File upload failed"
- PDF may be too large
- **Solution**: 
  1. Use Chrome's "Save as PDF" (creates smaller files)
  2. Or compress PDF at smallpdf.com
  3. Maximum file size: 100 MB
