Traditional Method: Print Full Journal

  1. Click "Open Journal for Printing" button above to open the journal
  2. Press Ctrl+P (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+P (Mac) to print
  3. Set paper size to 5.5" × 8.5" (Custom size in most printers)
  4. Select "Color" if possible (or Black & White works too)
  5. Enable "Background Graphics" or "Print Backgrounds" option
  6. Print all pages or select specific ranges

💡 Pro Tip: Save as PDF First

Instead of printing directly, we recommend saving as PDF first. This ensures consistent formatting and lets you print multiple copies later without regenerating:

  • In the print dialog, select "Save as PDF" or "Microsoft Print to PDF"
  • Save the file as OpenTransition-Journal.pdf
  • Print from the PDF viewer with custom paper size

🎨 VistaPrint DIY Flyer System

This journal now uses a DIY flyer-based approach perfect for VistaPrint! Since VistaPrint requires minimum 25 copies per page design, you can:

Benefits: Professional quality, bulk savings, personalized journals, enough for multiple copies!

See the VistaPrint DIY Guide for complete step-by-step instructions.

Quick Steps for VistaPrint DIY System:

  1. Choose page types: Decide which pages you want (e.g., photo tracking, calendar, notes)
  2. Generate PDFs: Create separate PDF for each page type at 5.75" × 8.75" (with bleed)
  3. Upload each type: Go to vistaprint.com/flyers, select 5.5" × 8.5" size
  4. Order 25+ copies: Minimum 25 per page design - enough for multiple journals!
  5. Choose options: 100 lb matte cover, matte finish (allows writing!), 3-hole punch (recommended)
  6. Assemble journals: Mix and match pages when they arrive to create custom journals

📐 Paper Size Options

Recommended: Half-Letter (5.5" × 8.5")

This is the ideal size for portability and convenience. If you don't have pre-cut paper:

  • Option 1: Print on standard letter paper and cut in half
  • Option 2: Take the PDF to a print shop (FedEx Office, Staples, etc.)
  • Option 3: Use your printer's custom paper size settings

🎨 Paper Recommendations

  • 20-24 lb paper: Standard weight, economical
  • 28-32 lb paper: Heavier, more premium feel
  • Cardstock: For cover page only (65-110 lb)

📚 Binding Suggestions

Once printed, you'll want to organize your pages. Here are some options:

Best Options:

  • 3-Ring Binder: Punch holes and use a small 5.5" × 8.5" binder (easiest to add/remove pages)
  • Spiral Binding: Take to a print shop for professional spiral binding
  • Folder/Portfolio: Keep loose pages in a decorative folder
  • DIY Binding: Use ribbon, string, or creative binding methods

📋 What's Included

The journal contains approximately 30 pages with the following sections:

  • 📸 Photo Tracking Pages: 5 face, 3 body, 3 custom area pages
  • 🎯 Milestone Pages: Medical, legal, social, and personal victories
  • 📊 Comparison Pages: Side-by-side progress views
  • 📅 Calendars: Monthly calendar templates
  • 💊 Tracking: Medication and appointment trackers
  • 💭 Reflection: Monthly reflection and goal-setting pages
  • 📝 Notes: Blank pages for free-form journaling
  • 📚 Resources: Contact and resource pages

💡 Usage Tips

  • Take consistent photos: Same lighting, angle, and distance for best comparisons
  • Print more pages as needed: You can print individual pages by selecting page ranges
  • Attach physical photos: Use photo corners or tape to attach printed photos
  • Make it personal: Decorate with stickers, colored pens, or drawings
  • Keep it private: Store in a secure location or with a trusted person
