Welcome to Your Journey

This printable journal is designed to help you document and celebrate your transition. Whether you're just starting or years into your journey, tracking your progress can be incredibly empowering and help you see how far you've come.

How to Use This Journal

📸 Photo Tracking Pages

Use these pages to attach photos or draw sketches of your progress. Take photos regularly (weekly or monthly) in similar lighting and angles to best see changes over time.

🎯 Milestone Pages

Record important events, medical appointments, legal changes, and personal celebrations. These moments are part of your story and deserve to be remembered.

📊 Progress Tracking

Use the comparison and calendar pages to track changes over time and stay organized with your appointments and medication schedules.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Take photos in consistent lighting for best comparisons
  • Use the same camera angle and distance each time
  • Write notes about how you're feeling alongside photos
  • Celebrate small victories - every change matters!
  • Keep this journal in a safe, private place

Privacy & Safety

This is your private journal. Keep it in a secure location where only you have access. If you're in a living situation where privacy is a concern, consider:

  • Keeping it with a trusted friend
  • Storing it in a locked box or safe
  • Using the digital OpenTransition app with app lock enabled

Getting Started

Setting Up Your Journal

Step 1: Print This Journal

Home/Office Printing: Print these pages on 5.5" x 8.5" paper (half-letter size). You can print double-sided to save paper, or single-sided if you prefer thicker pages. Consider using heavier paper (24lb+) for durability.

Professional Printing (VistaPrint DIY): Order individual page types as 5.5" x 8.5" matte flyers (minimum 25 per type). Choose which pages you want, then build custom journals from your flyer sets. See instructions.html or PROFESSIONAL-PRINTING.md for detailed ordering guide.

Step 2: Organize Your Pages

Use a binder, folder, or binding service to keep your pages together. This makes it easy to add new tracking pages as you continue your journey.

Step 3: Start Tracking

Begin with your first photo and milestone entries. Don't worry about making it perfect - this is your personal journal and there's no wrong way to use it.

What's Included

✨ Remember

Your transition is unique to you. Use this journal in whatever way feels right. Some people track daily, others monthly - find what works for you!
