# OpenTransition - App Description for Google Play Store

## Short Description (80 characters max)

Track your transition journey with photos and milestones privately & securely.

## Full Description

**OpenTransition** is a thoughtfully designed app for transgender individuals to document and track their transition journey through photos and milestones. Keep your progress private, secure, and organized—all in one place.

### 🌈 Your Journey, Your Privacy

OpenTransition puts **you in control**. Every photo, every milestone, every moment of your journey stays private on your device. With optional cloud backup, app lock, and even a disguised app icon, your transition documentation is truly yours alone.

### 📸 Visual Progress Tracking

**Take photos with confidence:**
- Track different body areas separately (face, body, custom areas)
- Compare photos side-by-side to see your changes over time
- Organize photos by date automatically
- View your journey in a beautiful gallery

### 🎯 Milestone Celebrations

**Remember every important moment:**
- Record significant events (starting HRT, coming out, name changes, etc.)
- Add notes and dates to each milestone
- Associate photos with specific achievements
- Look back and celebrate how far you've come

### 🔒 Privacy & Security Features

**Your data is protected:**
- **App Lock**: Secure with PIN or pattern
- **Disguised Mode**: Hide the app with a train-themed icon
- **Local Storage**: Data stays on your device by default
- **Optional Cloud Backup**: Sync securely with your Google account when you choose
- **No Tracking**: We don't collect or sell your personal information

### 🎨 Customizable Experience

**Make it yours:**
- Choose from multiple color themes (Pink, Blue, Purple, Green)
- Organize photos your way
- Create custom milestone types
- Dark mode friendly interface

### 💾 Data You Control

**Your data, your rules:**
- Export all your data anytime as a backup file
- Import data to restore or move to a new device
- Cloud sync is completely optional
- Delete everything with one tap if needed

### 🆓 Free & Open Source

OpenTransition is completely **free to use** with optional ads that support development. It's also **open source**, meaning the community can review the code, contribute features, and ensure your privacy is respected.

### ✨ Perfect For:

- Tracking hormone therapy (HRT) progress
- Documenting facial/body changes
- Recording important transition milestones
- Celebrating your journey privately
- Sharing progress with healthcare providers (optional)

### 🤝 Made for the Community, by the Community

OpenTransition is a fork of TransTracks, repackaged to be independently maintained and improved by community contributors. Your feedback and suggestions help make the app better for everyone.

---

**Important Notes:**

- This app requires Android 5.0 or higher
- Camera permission is optional (for taking photos directly in app)
- Storage permission is needed to import existing photos
- Internet permission is only used for optional cloud sync and Firebase services
- The app works completely offline if you choose

**Privacy First:** We believe your transition journey is deeply personal. That's why OpenTransition is designed with privacy at its core. No forced accounts, no tracking, no selling your data—just a simple, secure tool to help you document your journey on your own terms.
