# Privacy Policy

**Effective date / Last updated:** December 10, 2025  
**Application:** OpenTransition (`com.shelbeely.opentransition`)

OpenTransition is a self-tracking app for documenting transition-related photos and milestones. We designed it to work privately on your device with optional cloud backup. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

## Data We Collect

- **Content you add**: Photos, milestones, notes, and settings you save in the app. These stay **on your device by default** and are not uploaded unless you turn on cloud backup.
- **Cloud backup & sync (optional)**: If you enable backup, your data (photos, milestones, settings) is stored in your Google account via Firebase Authentication, Firestore/Storage. Your Google account ID/email is used for sign-in.
- **Analytics (optional)**: App usage events and device/app identifiers are collected by Firebase Analytics to improve stability and features. You can toggle analytics off in Settings.
- **Crash reports (optional)**: Crash logs with device details (model, OS version, stack traces) are sent to Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose issues. You can toggle crash reporting off in Settings.
- **Advertising data**: The app uses Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and coarse location to serve ads. We do **not** access your personal content for ads and do not sell data.

## How We Use Data

- Provide core functionality (saving photos/milestones, themes, settings)
- Optional backup/sync when you sign in with Google
- Diagnose issues and improve performance (analytics & crash reports)
- Show ads to support development
- Maintain security (fraud/abuse prevention)

## Data Sharing & Transfers

- We **do not sell** your data.
- Data may be processed by Google services you opt into:
  - Firebase Authentication, Firestore/Storage (cloud backup)
  - Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (diagnostics)
  - Google AdMob (ads)
- No other third parties receive your personal data.

## Storage, Retention, and Security

- **Local-first**: Your content stays on-device unless you enable cloud backup.
- **Retention**: Local data remains until you delete it or uninstall. Cloud data remains until you delete it or request removal. Diagnostics and ads data follow Google/Firebase retention policies.
- **Security**: Data in transit uses HTTPS. The app offers PIN/pattern lock and a disguised icon mode for additional privacy. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Your Choices & Controls

- Use the app fully **without an account** and **offline**.
- Toggle **Analytics** and **Crash Reports** in **Settings → Privacy**.
- Turn **Cloud Backup** on/off anytime; when off, new data stays on-device.
- Delete data directly in the app (remove photos/milestones) or uninstall to clear local data. If you enabled cloud backup, disable it and contact us to request cloud data removal.
- Export/back up your data from the app at any time.

## Children

OpenTransition is **not directed to children** and is intended for adults (18+). The app should not be used by anyone under 18.

## Health Disclaimer

OpenTransition is for personal tracking and informational purposes only. It does **not** provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical guidance.
