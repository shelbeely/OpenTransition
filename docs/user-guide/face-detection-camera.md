# Using the Face Detection Camera

## Overview

OpenTransition includes an intelligent camera feature that helps you take consistent progress photos. The camera uses face detection to provide real-time guidance for proper positioning.

## How It Works

When you take a photo using the camera:

1. **Position yourself** in front of the camera
2. **Watch for green indicators** showing proper alignment
3. **Follow the guidance text** for adjustments
4. **Capture when aligned** for consistent framing

## Visual Guides

### Face Box
- **Green box** = Perfect alignment
- **Yellow box** = Needs adjustment

### Guidance Messages

The camera will show you helpful messages:

- **"Chin up"** / **"Chin down"** - Adjust head angle
- **"Turn left"** / **"Turn right"** - Face the camera
- **"Level your head"** - Straighten your head
- **"Move closer"** / **"Move back"** - Adjust distance
- **"✓ Perfect alignment"** - Ready to capture!

### Other Guides

- **Center line** - Keep your face centered
- **Shoulder guide** - Align your shoulders
- **Landmark dots** - Show detected facial features

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Good Lighting**
   - Face a window or light source
   - Avoid harsh shadows
   - Consistent lighting for comparison photos

2. **Consistent Distance**
   - The camera tracks face size
   - Move closer/back when prompted
   - Same distance = better comparisons

3. **Head Position**
   - Face forward
   - Keep head level
   - Neutral expression works best

4. **Privacy Controls**
   - Tap the eye icon to hide/show guides
   - All processing happens on your device
   - No face data is sent anywhere

## Taking a Photo

1. From **Home** or **Gallery**, tap the camera button
2. Position yourself using the guides
3. Wait for **"Perfect alignment"** message
4. Tap the **large white button** to capture
5. Review and save your photo

## Need Help?

- **No face detected?** Ensure your face is visible and well-lit
- **Guides too distracting?** Toggle them off with the eye icon
- **Different angles?** The guides adapt to your position
- **Camera not starting?** Check camera permissions in Settings

## Privacy & Safety

✓ **On-device processing** - No cloud upload
✓ **No face recognition** - Only pose detection
✓ **Optional guides** - Use what helps you
✓ **Your control** - Delete any photo anytime

The face detection feature is designed to be supportive and helpful, not judgmental. Use it in whatever way makes you comfortable!
