# Features Overview

OpenTransition provides comprehensive tools for tracking your transition journey. This page gives you an overview of all the app's features.

## Core Features

### 📸 Photo Tracking
Document your transition with photos organized by type:
- **Face Photos**: Track facial changes from HRT
- **Body Photos**: Full body progress tracking
- **Custom Areas**: Track specific body parts or areas

[Learn more about photo management →](photos.md)

### 🎤 Voice Tracking
Record and analyze your voice to track vocal changes:
- **Audio Recording**: Record voice samples with built-in recorder
- **Voice Analysis**: Automatic formant analysis (F1, F2, F3, F4)
- **Pitch Tracking**: Monitor fundamental frequency (F0) changes
- **Progress Visualization**: View waveforms, pitch progression, and formant charts
- **Comparisons**: Compare recordings over time to see vocal progress

Voice tracking helps monitor changes in vocal resonance and pitch, which are important aspects of voice feminization or masculinization during transition.

### 🎯 Milestone Tracking
Record important events in your journey:
- Medical milestones (HRT start, surgeries)
- Legal changes (name, gender marker)
- Social milestones (coming out)
- Personal celebrations

[Learn more about milestones →](milestones.md)

### 🖼️ Gallery & Comparison
- Browse all your photos in a organized gallery
- Compare photos side-by-side
- Filter by type and date
- See your progress over time

### 🔒 Privacy & Security
- **App Lock**: PIN or pattern protection
- **Train Mode**: Disguised app icon
- **Local Storage**: Data stays on your device
- **No Tracking**: Your data is yours alone

[Learn more about privacy →](privacy.md)

### 💾 Backup & Sync
- **Local Backups**: Export data as `.ttbackup` files
- **Cloud Sync**: Optional Firebase backup
- **Easy Restore**: Transfer to new devices easily

[Learn more about backups →](backup.md)

### 🎨 Customization
- Multiple color themes (Pink, Blue, Purple, Green)
- Flexible photo organization
- Custom milestone types
- Personalized experience
