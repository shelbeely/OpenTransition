# Taking & Managing Photos and Audio

*This page is under development. Check back soon for detailed photo and audio management instructions.*

## Quick Tips

### Photos

- Take photos in consistent lighting
- Use the same angle and distance
- Take photos regularly for best tracking
- Organize by type (Face, Body)

### Audio Recordings

- Record in a quiet environment
- Maintain consistent microphone distance
- Record similar phrases or sounds for comparison
- Take recordings regularly to track vocal progress

For now, see the [Getting Started Guide](getting-started.md#taking-your-first-photo) for basic instructions.

## Voice Tracking

OpenTransition includes voice tracking features to help you monitor vocal changes during transition:

- **Record Audio Samples**: Use the built-in audio recorder to capture voice samples
- **Automatic Analysis**: Get instant formant analysis (F1, F2, F3, F4) and pitch data
- **Track Progress**: Compare recordings over time to see vocal changes
- **Visualizations**: View waveforms, pitch progression, and formant charts

Voice tracking is particularly useful for:
- Monitoring pitch changes during voice training
- Tracking formant frequencies for voice feminization or masculinization
- Documenting progress with speech therapists
- Celebrating vocal milestones

See the developer [Voice Tracking documentation](../features/voice-tracking.md) for technical details.
