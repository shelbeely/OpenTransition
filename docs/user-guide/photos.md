# Taking & Managing Photos

Learn how to take high-quality, consistent photos to track your transition progress effectively.

## Enhanced Camera Features

OpenTransition includes professional camera controls to help you take better tracking photos:

### Camera Controls

- **Flash**: Toggle between Off, On, and Auto modes for proper lighting
- **Camera Switch**: Easily switch between front (selfie) and rear cameras
- **Grid Overlay**: Enable a rule-of-thirds grid for better photo alignment
- **Self-Timer**: Set a 3s, 5s, or 10s timer for hands-free photos
- **Photo Comparison Overlay**: View a ghost image of your previous photo to maintain consistent positioning

### Using the Photo Comparison Overlay

The photo comparison overlay is a powerful feature for tracking face and body changes:

1. **Taking Your First Photo**: Simply take a photo as normal
2. **Taking Follow-up Photos**: 
   - When you take another photo of the same type (face/body), the camera can show your previous photo as a semi-transparent overlay
   - Adjust the opacity slider to make the overlay more or less visible
   - Align your current position with the ghost image for consistent tracking
3. **Benefits**:
   - Maintain the same head angle and position (face photos)
   - Keep consistent body posture and camera distance (body photos)
   - Ensure comparable lighting and background
   - Make progress more visible and accurate

## Tips for Best Results

### Face Photos
- Take photos in consistent lighting
- Use the same angle and distance from the camera
- Use the overlay to match your head position exactly
- Consider using the timer for stability
- Enable the grid to keep your face centered

### Body Photos
- Use the timer to step back from the camera
- Wear similar clothing for easier comparison
- Use the grid and overlay to maintain consistent posture
- Stand at the same distance from the camera
- Use consistent background and lighting

### General Tips
- Take photos regularly (weekly or monthly works well)
- Use the same time of day for consistent lighting
- Save date and location in photo metadata when possible
- Organize by type (Face, Body, Custom) for easy comparison

## Quick Photo Guide

1. **Open the Camera**: Tap the camera icon when adding a photo
2. **Adjust Settings**: 
   - Toggle grid if you want alignment help
   - Set timer if taking hands-free
   - Enable overlay if available (appears automatically when you have a previous photo)
3. **Position Yourself**: Use the grid and/or overlay to position yourself
4. **Take the Photo**: Tap the capture button or wait for timer
5. **Review & Save**: Photo is automatically processed and ready to assign

## Managing Your Photos

- **View Gallery**: Browse all your photos organized by type and date
- **Compare Photos**: Select multiple photos to view side-by-side
- **Edit Photos**: Crop, rotate, or adjust as needed
- **Organize**: Tag photos with milestones or custom categories
- **Export**: Backup or share your progress securely

For more detailed photo management instructions, see the [Getting Started Guide](getting-started.md#taking-your-first-photo).
