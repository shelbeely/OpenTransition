# Getting Started with OpenTransition

Welcome to **OpenTransition**! This guide will help you set up and start using the app to track your transition journey.

## What is OpenTransition?

OpenTransition is a free, private app designed specifically for transgender individuals to document their transition through photos and milestones. Think of it as your personal, secure transition diary that lives on your phone.

## Why Use OpenTransition?

✅ **Private & Secure** - Your photos and data stay on your device  
✅ **Easy to Use** - Simple interface designed for everyone  
✅ **Free** - No subscription fees, no hidden costs  
✅ **Your Data** - Export, backup, or delete anytime  
✅ **Optional Cloud Sync** - Backup to your Google account if you want  

## First Time Setup

### 1. Download & Install

Download OpenTransition from the Google Play Store and install it on your Android device (requires Android 5.0 or higher).

### 2. Open the App

When you first open OpenTransition, you'll see the home screen. No account required to start using the app!

### 3. Grant Permissions (Optional)

The app may ask for permissions:

- **Camera**: To take photos directly in the app (you can skip this and import photos instead)
- **Microphone**: To record voice samples for voice tracking (optional)
- **Storage/Photos**: To import existing photos from your device
- **Notifications**: To remind you to take progress photos (optional)

!!! tip "Permissions are Optional"
    You can use the app without granting permissions. Just import photos manually when you want to add them.

## Taking Your First Photo

### Option 1: Take a Photo with Camera

1. Tap the **➕ button**
2. Select **Take Photo**
3. Choose photo type: **Face** or **Body**
   - Use flexibly for any area you want to track
4. Take your photo
5. Select the date (defaults to today)
6. Save!

### Option 2: Import Existing Photos

1. Tap the **➕ button**
2. Select **Import Photos**
3. Choose photo type: **Face** or **Body**
   - Use flexibly for any area you want to track
4. Select one or more photos from your device
5. Choose the date for each photo
6. Save!

!!! tip "Photo Types"
    - **Face**: For facial progress (great for tracking HRT facial changes)
    - **Body**: For full body progress photos
    
    You can use these types flexibly to track any areas you want. For example, you might use "Face" for close-up photos of specific features, or "Body" for any part of your body.

## Recording Your Voice

OpenTransition includes voice tracking to help monitor vocal changes during transition.

### Recording Audio

1. Tap the **➕ button**
2. Select **Record Audio**
3. Grant microphone permission if prompted
4. Tap the **Record** button to start recording
5. Speak or read a consistent phrase (helps with comparison later)
6. Tap **Stop** when finished
7. Review the recording
8. Select the date (defaults to today)
9. Save!

!!! tip "Voice Recording Tips"
    - Record in a quiet environment
    - Use the same phrase or vowel sounds each time
    - Maintain consistent microphone distance
    - Record at the same time of day when possible
    - The app automatically analyzes pitch (F0) and formant frequencies (F1-F4)

### Understanding Voice Analysis

After recording, the app provides:
- **Pitch (F0)**: Your average speaking pitch in Hz
- **Formants (F1, F2, F3, F4)**: Vocal tract resonance frequencies
- **Waveform**: Visual representation of your recording
- **Duration**: Length of the recording

These metrics help you track vocal changes over time, which is valuable for voice training and monitoring HRT effects on voice.

## Adding Your First Milestone

Milestones help you remember important events in your transition.

1. Tap the **➕ button**
2. Select **Add Milestone**
3. Enter a title (e.g., "Started HRT", "First therapy session", "Changed name legally")
4. Add details (optional)
5. Set the date
6. Save!

### Common Milestones to Track

- 🏥 Medical events (HRT start date, surgeries, doctor appointments)
- 📝 Legal changes (name change, gender marker changes)
- 💬 Social milestones (coming out events, first day presenting)
- 🎉 Personal celebrations (first voice pass, first time using public restroom)

## Viewing Your Progress

### Gallery View

1. Tap **Gallery** in the bottom navigation
2. Browse all your photos and audio recordings organized by date
3. Filter by type (Face, Body, Audio)
4. Tap any photo to view it full screen
5. Tap any audio recording to play it and see voice analysis

!!! note "Photo Types"
    The gallery supports Face photos, Body photos, and Audio recordings. Custom areas can be tracked using Face or Body types.

### Compare Photos and Voice Recordings

1. Open any photo or audio in gallery
2. Swipe left or right to see other items
3. Use the comparison view to see changes over time
4. For audio: Compare formant charts and pitch data side-by-side
5. Items are automatically organized by date for easy comparison

### Timeline View

1. View your milestones in chronological order
2. See which photos are associated with milestones
3. Track your journey month by month

## Staying Organized

### Photo Organization Tips

- Take photos in similar lighting for better comparison
- Use the same angle and distance each time
- Take photos on a regular schedule (weekly, monthly, etc.)
- Label custom areas clearly (e.g., "Hands", "Legs", "Hair")

### Milestone Organization Tips

- Be specific with milestone titles
- Add notes about how you felt or what happened
- Include relevant medical information (dosages, medications)
- Link photos to milestones when appropriate

## Privacy & Security

### Using App Lock

Protect your photos with a PIN or pattern:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Tap **Security**
3. Enable **App Lock**
4. Choose PIN or Pattern
5. Set your code

Now the app will lock when you close it!

### Disguised Mode (Train Icon)

Want extra privacy? Enable "Train Mode":

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Tap **Security**
3. Enable **Train Mode**
4. The app icon changes to look like a train app

Perfect for keeping your transition journey private!

### Cloud Backup (Optional)

You can optionally backup your data to Google:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Tap **Backup & Sync**
3. Sign in with Google
4. Enable **Cloud Sync**

Your data will now backup automatically!

!!! warning "Backup is Optional"
    You don't need to create an account or use cloud backup. The app works perfectly fine storing everything locally on your device.

## Backing Up Your Data

### Local Backup (Recommended)

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Tap **Backup & Sync**
3. Tap **Export Data**
4. Choose where to save the backup file
5. Keep this file safe!

This creates a `.ttbackup` file containing all your photos and data.

### Restoring from Backup

1. Open OpenTransition
2. Find your `.ttbackup` file
3. Tap the file to open it with OpenTransition
4. Confirm you want to import
5. Wait for import to complete

!!! tip "Regular Backups"
    We recommend creating a backup monthly and storing it in a safe place (like Google Drive, Dropbox, or an external drive).
