## Short Written Intro

**OpenTransition** is a private, secure Android app for documenting a gender transition journey.
Built by [Shelbee](https://github.com/shelbeely) (`shelbeely`) as the active successor to the
beloved **TransTracks** app — originally created by Cassie Wilson and retired from the Google Play
Store in 2025 — OpenTransition carries that mission forward under the same GPL v3 license.

Take dated photos sorted into face, body, or custom albums. Log milestones that matter to you.
Record voice samples to revisit over time. Browse your gallery and see how far you've come — all
with strong privacy features and optional database encryption to make sure your journey stays yours.

Available for Android phones, tablets, and Wear OS smartwatches.

### 60-Second Script

> Hi, I'm Shelbee — also known online as `shelbeely` — and I'm the developer of OpenTransition.
>
> OpenTransition is a private, secure Android app designed to help you document your gender
> transition journey in one place. It started as a fork of the original TransTracks app, which was
> created single-handedly by Cassie Wilson and meant everything to a lot of people before it was
> retired in 2025. I built OpenTransition to make sure that tool — and the community that relied on
> it — wouldn't lose something so personal.
>
> With OpenTransition you can take dated photos and organize them into face, body, or custom albums.
> A built-in camera uses face detection to help you frame face photos consistently over time. You
> can log milestones — the big moments and the quiet ones alike — and record voice samples to listen
> back to later. There's even a Wear OS companion app so you can trigger a photo or check your
> milestones right from your wrist.
>
> Privacy is central to the app. You can lock it with a PIN, pattern, or biometrics, enable a
> disguised mode, or set up a decoy vault. Your database can be fully encrypted with SQLCipher.
> Nothing is uploaded or shared without your explicit action.
>
> If you were a TransTracks user, you can import your `.ttbackup` file directly — all your photos,
> milestones, and data come with you.
>
> OpenTransition is free, open-source under GPL v3, and built with love for the trans community.
> Come find us on GitHub at github.com/shelbeely/OpenTransition.
